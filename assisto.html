How to playAssisto · five-a-side, snooker rules

The assist

The ball must strike one of your own players before it touches anything else. After that it can do as it likes — carom off a defender, off the boards, into the net. That is the whole game: pass, then finish.

ON: RED tells you whose players are legal to hit, exactly as snooker tells you which ball is on. Legal players wear a bright collar and a target ring; the other side fades back.

Fouls

  • You strike an opponent first.
  • You touch nobody and the ball barely moves — not a real attempt.
  • You run out of time on the shot clock without playing.
  • A goal scored from any of the above is disallowed.

A foul gives your opponent a free shot: for that one shot anything is legal, so they can fire straight at goal. The mouth lights up amber when it is on. Three fouls in a row concede a penalty from the spot.

Not fouls

  • A properly struck shot that threads everyone: you simply lose the turn.
  • Ball resting against your own player? You are deemed to have hit it — play away.
  • Ball resting against an opponent? That contact is ignored, not held against you.
  • An own goal always counts, foul or not. Mind your own net.

The nudge

Find your own player and you earn a nudge: that player may move up to two of its own widths. Foul and you get nothing. A player may only move within its own part of the pitch, may not crowd a team-mate so tightly the ball cannot pass, and may not slide up against an opponent or onto the ball. Your keeper can be nudged too, but only along its own goal line. Tap the ringed player and drag, or tap anywhere else to decline.

The break

Every shot that finds your own player extends your break, shown beside the foul pips. A foul or a shot that touches nobody ends it.

Controls

Drag anywhere and pull back, like a slingshot — the pull is measured from your finger, not from the ball, so you can do it out in open space. Run out of screen? Hold at the edge and the power keeps winding up. The dotted line traces about a third of the pitch, including the first deflection; the rest is yours to read.

Your team

Five outfield players plus a keeper. Your shape guards your own goal and gives you the outlets to attack with, so a heavy defence is safe but short of passing options. Change it under Your tactic.
