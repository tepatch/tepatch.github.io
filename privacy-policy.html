Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8th 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android game 2048 League: Daily Puzzles (formerly 2048: Puzzle of Twos; the same app under a new name), published on Google Play by Nenad Rakovic. In this policy, "the game", "we" and "us" refer to that app and its developer.

We are committed to respecting your privacy and the integrity and security of your personal data. This policy explains what the game collects, why, who else can see it, and how you can delete it.

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we revise the date at the top. If a change is material, we make reasonable efforts to tell you, for example with an in-game notice. By installing or using the game you acknowledge the collection and use of information as described here.

1. What data we collect

What we collect depends on the features you use. The game is fully playable as a guest; signing in is optional.

A. Data you provide

  • Google sign-in (optional) - if you sign in with Google, we receive the email address and display name of the Google account you choose. Both are stored with your account and used only to sign you in; neither is shown in the game or to other players.
  • Profile choices - the avatar, title, frame, board style and effect you select in the game.
  • Contact - your email address and the content of your message, only if you contact us (for support, feedback, or a deletion request).

B. Data collected automatically

When you play, the game and the service providers listed in section 4 collect:

  • Gameplay data - scores, biggest tile reached, numbers of moves and undos, board sizes played, the date, time and length of your sessions, daily-challenge, campaign and streak progress, coins earned and spent, cosmetics unlocked, league tier and rating, and, for rated league games only, the full list of moves you made (see section 3).
  • Device and technical data - an app-installation identifier generated by Firebase, your IP address (used to derive an approximate location such as your country), device manufacturer and model, operating system version, language and app version. The current version of the game does not request the Android advertising identifier. Versions before 6.00 could receive it through the Firebase Analytics SDK; you can reset it at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Crash and diagnostic data - if the game crashes or hits an unexpected error, technical details about the error and the device state at that moment, so we can fix it.
  • User ID - analytics and crash reports (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics) are linked to your Firebase user ID, the random account identifier described in section 6, so we can count returning players and group crashes. It is not your name or email address.

C. Notifications

The game can send an optional daily reminder. It is off until you turn it on, and on Android 13 and newer it also needs the notification permission, which you can revoke in system settings. The reminder is scheduled on your device; no data leaves the device for it.

D. No advertising

The game shows no advertisements and contains no advertising SDKs. We do not share your data with any advertising network, and we do not use your data for advertising.

2. Why we collect and use your data

We process this data to:

  • run, provide and maintain the game and keep your progress across sessions and devices
  • recognise you when you return, and merge a guest profile into your account when you sign in
  • show your scores in the leaderboards, your results in the weekly seasons and the rated league, and let other players verify rated games (section 3)
  • understand how the game is used, in aggregate, so we can improve it
  • find and fix crashes and errors
  • respond to your messages and provide player support
  • honour your deletion request (section 7)

3. What other players can see

Some of your data is public to other players of the game, because competing against each other is what the leaderboards and the league are for. The game shows no names: you appear to others only as your avatar and title, next to your results. Any player of the game can see:

  • Your leaderboard entries - score, biggest tile, number of moves, number of undos, duration and date of each listed game, on every board size.
  • Your weekly season rows - best score, biggest tile and number of moves for the week, with your rank.
  • Your rated league row - tier, rating, number of attempts, and the sum and best of your counted scores for the week, with your rank.
  • Your rated games as move lists. A rated game is published as the complete sequence of moves you made, together with its score and duration. Other players can replay any rated game move by move in the game's replay viewer; this is how the game verifies that scores are genuine. Only rated league games are recorded this way. Casual games, daily challenges and campaign levels are never recorded as move lists.

Your name, email address and device data are never available to other players. Your coins and cosmetics inventory are never displayed to other players in the game.

4. How we share your data

We do not sell your data. The game is built on Google Firebase, and the following Firebase and Google services process data on our behalf:

  • Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In - to create and sign in your account
  • Cloud Firestore - to store your profile, statistics, scores, coins, cosmetics, season and league rows
  • Google Analytics for Firebase - to measure how the game is used, in aggregate
  • Firebase Crashlytics - to collect crash and error reports
  • Firebase Remote Config - to deliver game settings (for example economy values and the league schedule); it stores no data about you
  • Cloud Functions for Firebase - used only to delete your data when you delete your account (section 7)
  • Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity - may be used to confirm that requests come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the game installed from Google Play

These providers process data under Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. For how Google uses data from apps that use its services, see this page.

Beyond those providers, we disclose data only if the law requires it.

5. International data transfers

The game is available worldwide, and the Firebase services above may store and process your data in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States. Google maintains safeguards for such transfers as described in its privacy policy.

6. Data retention

Your profile, statistics, scores, coins, cosmetics, season and league rows are stored under an account identifier - a random guest identifier by default, or your Google account if you sign in - for as long as that account exists.

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google Firebase under its retention settings for this project (user-level analytics data at most 14 months, crash reports 90 days). They are linked to your Firebase user ID (section 1).

Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything tied to it at any time, in either of two ways. Both are described step by step on our account-deletion page.

  • In the game - on the home screen, ⋮ Menu (top-right) → Delete account. If it has been a long time since you signed in, you are asked to sign in with Google again first. The account is deleted right away, and the data below is removed automatically within minutes.
  • By email, without reinstalling the game - write to rakovic.nenad+2048@gmail.com from the Google email address you used to sign in; that address is what identifies your account to us. We verify that the request comes from the account's owner, then reply and delete the account and its data within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes: the account itself, your profile and statistics, your coins and cosmetics, your leaderboard scores on every board size, your weekly season rows, your league rows including the published rated games, and the records created when a guest profile was merged into your account. This cannot be undone.

Analytics and crash reports are linked to your user ID and are not removed when the account is deleted; they expire as described in section 6 (at most 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports). Nothing else tied to your account is kept. Guest data that was never linked to a sign-in is stored under a random identifier that does not identify you, and nothing in it could tell us which guest you were; uninstalling the game or clearing its storage severs your device from it for good. If you sign in with Google, the guest data becomes part of your account and is deleted with it.

8. Children's privacy

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. You can exercise deletion yourself as described in section 7; for anything else, contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.
