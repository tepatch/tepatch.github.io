Delete your account

2048 League: Daily Puzzles (formerly 2048: Puzzle of Twos) · developer: Nenad Rakovic

You can delete your 2048 League account and everything tied to it at any time. There are two ways to do it. Both delete the same data, and both are free.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open the game. On the home screen, tap the ⋮ Menu button in the top-right corner.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If it has been a long time since you signed in, the game asks you to sign in with Google again first; that is a security step required before an account can be deleted.

Your account is deleted right away, and the data listed below is removed automatically within minutes. The game continues as a new guest profile with nothing carried over.

Delete account is shown only when you are signed in with Google. A guest profile that was never signed in has no account to delete; see guest data below.

Option 2 — By email (no reinstall needed)

If you no longer have the game installed, or you cannot sign in, send a deletion request by email:

Email a deletion request

or write to rakovic.nenad+2048@gmail.com.

So that we can find and verify the account, send the request from the Google email address you used to sign in. That address is the only thing that identifies your account to us. If you can no longer use it, tell us which address you signed in with and we will confirm the request with you before deleting anything. We only delete an account once we are confident the request comes from its owner.

We reply to every request and delete the account and its data within 30 days of receiving it. Most requests are handled much sooner.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your account itself (the Firebase Authentication user linked to your Google sign-in)
  • your profile and statistics: avatar, title, frame, level, wins, points, best tiles and move counts (the player name is part of the account and goes with it)
  • your coins and cosmetics, including anything unlocked or bought with coins
  • your leaderboard scores on every board size
  • your weekly season rows
  • your league rows, including the published rated games (the move lists other players could replay)
  • the account-upgrade records created when a guest profile was merged into your account

Deletion cannot be undone. Coins and cosmetics are not refunded and cannot be transferred to another account.

What is kept

Two kinds of data are not removed when your account is deleted:

  • Analytics and crash reports. They are linked to your Firebase user ID (a random identifier, not your name or email address) and are not deleted with the account. They expire under Google Firebase's retention periods: at most 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports (see the privacy policy).
  • Guest data. If you only ever played as a guest and never signed in with Google, your scores are stored under a random identifier that is not linked to you as a person, so there is no account to delete and nothing that could identify the data as yours. Clearing the game's storage on your device, or uninstalling it, removes the device's link to that identifier for good. If you sign in with Google first, the guest data becomes part of your account and is deleted with it.

Questions

Anything unclear, or a request that does not fit either route? Write to rakovic.nenad+2048@gmail.com. The full details of what the game stores, and why, are in the Privacy Policy.
